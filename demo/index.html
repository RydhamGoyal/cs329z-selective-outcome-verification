STANFORD CS 329Z · RESEARCH DEMO

When should an agent check its work?

AI agents can change customer orders and account details through tools. A tool may report success even when the saved record differs from what the customer asked for. If the agent continues, that mistake can affect later steps.

Checking every change takes extra time and tool calls. I am testing whether the agent can check the changes that matter most and still complete tasks reliably with fewer checks.

This midpoint demo presents five guided examples from the working system. Three come from benchmark tasks. Two deliberately introduce an address error to test whether checking finds it. Results summarizes the 70 natural benchmark runs. These early results do not yet establish a reliability gain.

What the fault test means

The controlled storage fault demonstrates that an independent readback can detect a wrong persisted address and prompt local repair. The benchmark evaluator replays tool arguments, so its reward does not score that injected storage fault.

Injected trials do not enter the official success rates above.
